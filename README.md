# paperskyline.org

The public website for **Paper Skyline** — the landing page, plus privacy, terms and support.

Hand-written static HTML and one stylesheet, zero JavaScript. No framework, no build step, no
dependencies. It is meant to load instantly, stay legible for years, and never break because a
toolchain moved on.

## Why it exists

Paper Skyline is on Google Play; the site says what it is and links there. It also carries the
privacy policy and terms the store listings point at.

## Structure

```
index.html  devlog.html  privacy.html  terms.html  support.html  404.html
cities/               # generated by tools/cities.py: one page per shipped city, plus cities/index.html
world/                # generated by tools/cities.py: world/, a hub per continent, a page per country
zh-Hans/  id/          # generated by tools/cities.py: the same cities/ and world/ pages in each language
devlog/  feed.xml      # generated by tools/news.py: a page per release and the Atom feed, beside devlog.html
video/                # the home page reel and its poster, copied from the game repo
styles.css            # tokens + every component, light/dark via prefers-color-scheme
img/                  # the game's paintings and keepers, exported by tools/art.py
brand/                # the app icon and the social card
tools/                # art.py, check.sh, cities.py + i18n.py, demand.py, news.py (devlog.html from the game's changelog), shots.sh
docs/superpowers/specs/               # the design spec this was built from
sitemap.xml           # an index: sitemap-pages.xml (by hand); sitemap-cities.xml, sitemap-world.xml, sitemap-zh-hans.xml, sitemap-id.xml (cities.py); sitemap-devlog.xml (news.py)
robots.txt  site.webmanifest  CNAME
```

Every colour and both typefaces come from the game's Paper Pop standard (`docs/UI_STANDARD.md`, V-D173):
navy, pink for the one primary, yellow, card stock, cream, sky and blue; sticker cards with a navy
border, white ring and hard navy shadow; Baloo 2 ExtraBold and Be Vietnam Pro. Dark mode is the same
stickers on a deep navy night.

## The art is the game's own

The hero and the social card use the game's Paris painting, and the keepers section shows all thirty
keepers of the fifteen shipped cities, each on a strip of its city's painting. `tools/art.py` reads them from the game repo and writes
`img/` and `brand/`, so re-run it after the game's art changes rather than editing images by hand. Each image is exported at the size its slot needs, as AVIF and WebP, with a JPEG
(paintings) or PNG (cut-out keepers) fallback for browsers that take neither.

"In the game" shows the five 1.9.0 Play captures from `docs/store/google-play/1.9.0/en-US/`, caption
band cropped, at 800 and 1600 px. `img/shot-paris-*` (business.html) is a frozen 1.3 export.
`brand/google-play-badge.png` is Google's official badge, used unmodified.

The step glyphs and the blueprint elevation are still inline `<svg>`.

## City pages

`tools/cities.py` writes `cities/<id>/` and `world/<continent>/<country>/` from the city graph and the Atlas text (#96); never edit them by hand.
A page is indexed and listed in its sitemap only with 300+ words of its own verified text (450+ Han characters in zh-Hans); below that it gets `noindex,follow`.
The zh-Hans and id pages take every city, keeper and country line from the game's locale packs; the page strings live in `tools/i18n.py`.
Extra verified text from `design/web-text/{cities,countries}/<key>.<lang>.md` in the game repo renders as one H2 per `##` section and counts toward the rule.
Every page lists all its languages as `hreflang` alternates, and `check.sh` fails any set that is not reciprocal.
Their share cards are `img/og-<slug>-<lang>.jpg` wherever the pack names the city differently; `tools/art.py` draws the Chinese ones in Noto Sans SC Black (OFL), fetched once into `tools/.tmp/`.
It also stamps the hand-written pages: the city count on `index.html` and `support.html`, and the Discord invite on every page.

## Discord

The invite lives in one constant, `DISCORD` in `tools/i18n.py`; after a swap, re-run `tools/cities.py`. `check.sh` fails any page whose footer lacks it.

## Devlog

`tools/news.py` turns the game's `docs/store/changelog.json` into `devlog.html`, `devlog/<version>/`, `feed.xml` and `sitemap-devlog.xml`.
Nothing newer than `LIVE` in news.py is published, so raise it only once that version is on Play production.
Run it on game main's changelog via `git show`, with the `google-play/whats-new-*.txt` files beside it, never on a checkout's working copy.

## Shipping a city

A city is published only once its version is on Play production (HCMC, Amsterdam and Singapore wait for 1.9.4).
Add its keepers to `CITIES` in `tools/art.py` (plus `NAMES`/`IDS` if its slug differs), then run `tools/art.py`, `tools/cities.py`, and `tools/news.py` with `LIVE` raised.
Then run `tools/check.sh`; country pages that fall under the word rule stay `noindex` until the City Writer adds verified text.

## City demand report

Once a month, `tools/demand.py <Search Console export zip>` counts each city's impressions from the Performance export (Queries and Pages), matching the city graph's en, zh-Hans and id names (#95).
A planned city is a signal at 100+ impressions in 28 days, or in the planned top 5 two months running; the month's top is kept in `tools/.tmp/demand/`. Fill a copy of `docs/demand/note-template.md` and file it in the game repo's `docs/validation/seo/`, not here: Pages publishes every file in this repo. Test: `python3 tools/test_demand.py`.

## Copy

One language per page: English at the root, zh-Hans and id generated under their prefixes (vi is left out). No em dash (—) in any page's copy, titles or meta: write a period, comma or colon, and `|` in page titles. `tools/check.sh` fails on one.

## Checks

Run before every commit:

```bash
tools/check.sh                        # every page: validity, links, zero-JS, unlinked badges, alt/width/height
tools/shots.sh index.html privacy.html  # 500/768/1280 px, light + dark, for a visual read
tools/art.py                          # re-export the paintings, keepers, social card and icons
```

500 px is the narrowest width this machine's headless Chrome will lay out, so mobile breakpoints
are written at ≥ 500 px to stay checkable here.

Headless Chrome has no `prefers-color-scheme` switch. `shots.sh` copies the site to `tools/.tmp/`
and rewrites the dark media query to `@media all` for the dark run — the page itself is untouched.

## Local preview

No server needed — open `index.html` in a browser. To serve it properly:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

## Deploying

GitHub Pages, from the default branch root:

1. Repo → **Settings → Pages** → Source: *Deploy from a branch* → `main` / `/ (root)`.
2. `CNAME` already declares `paperskyline.org`, so Pages picks the custom domain up.
3. At the registrar, point the apex at GitHub Pages:
   - `A` records → `185.199.108.153`, `185.199.109.153`, `185.199.110.153`, `185.199.111.153`
   - and a `CNAME` for `www` → `trunghieulam.github.io`
4. Back in Settings → Pages, tick **Enforce HTTPS** once the certificate is issued.

## Before publishing — things that still need a decision

1. **Contact.** Every page's mailto is the owner's address (user, 2026-09-28); it must receive mail
   before publishing, since store reviewers do check.
2. **Store links.** Google Play is linked (package `paperskyline.org`); `check.sh` fails any
   `store-link` that points elsewhere, and any Play link without the referrer
   `utm_source=site, utm_medium=<page>, utm_campaign=<slot>`. The App Store badge stays an unlinked `<span class="badge">`
   until the iPhone version ships, and `check.sh` fails it if it becomes a link.
3. **Developer and law.** The developer is "THLab (Trung Hieu Lam)", and the terms are governed by
   Vietnamese law (user, 2026-09-28).
4. **Two claims are absent on purpose**: price and a release date. The city count (ten) is stated.

## Keeping the policy honest

Privacy policy version 3 adds the opt-in AdMob rewarded ad on Android (F17): child-directed,
non-personalised, UMP consent in the EEA and UK, no advertising ID. Version 4 (branch web-stats-v4, G5)
describes Paper Skyline 1.1: made for adults 18 and over (V-D37), anonymous gameplay statistics to our
Cloudflare Worker, in-game feedback and `feedback.html`, a no-JS mailto form for adults. Version 2 described Paper Skyline 1.0: progress and settings saved on the device, no ads, analytics or purchases, no Unity cloud service enabled, and one-time HTTPS downloads of New York,
Tokyo, Cairo and Rio from Cloudflare R2 with no identifiers sent. It is the Play Console privacy URL,
so keep `privacy.html` at that path. Any change to the game's data flow bumps the policy **first**.
