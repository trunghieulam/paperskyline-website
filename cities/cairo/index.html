The city

The Victorious

The Fatimids founded Cairo in 969 and named it al-Qahira, "the Victorious". It grew beside older towns on the Nile, just where the river valley opens into the wide delta, and became one of the largest cities in Africa.

“I'd follow the Nile to where the delta begins.”

The landmark

The Great Pyramid of Giza

The Great Pyramid was built for King Khufu about 4,500 years ago from an estimated 2.3 million stone blocks. At about 146 metres it stayed the tallest structure people had built for some 3,800 years. It is the last of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World still standing.

Two keepers

Who keeps the wish in Cairo.

An Elite wakes first; the Boss guards the wish itself. Hit their weak points for a BONK!, land the last blow for a KO!, and each one grins and takes a bow.

  • The Elite

    The Camel

    Chews slowly, thinks slowly · Keeper of the Desert Road

    The camels that carry visitors around Giza today are newcomers there. When the pyramids rose, some 4,500 years ago, donkeys carried the builders' loads and boats moved the stone. Camels became common in Egypt, most historians think, only about two thousand years later, spreading with Assyrian, Persian and Greek rulers.

    The camel looks up from chewing. The camel grumbles, shifts its feet and goes on chewing. The camel folds its legs and sits down, very slowly.

    “I'd ride one right up to the pyramids.”

  • The Boss

    The Sphinx

    Dozing for 4,500 years · Keeper of Giza

    Carved from a single ridge of Giza's bedrock some 4,500 years ago, most likely for King Khafre, the Great Sphinx spent centuries buried to its shoulders in sand. A stone tablet between its paws tells how young Prince Thutmose dreamed it promised him the throne if he dug it free. He did, and became king.

    The Sphinx shakes the sand out of its wings. The Sphinx ruffles one wing and narrows its eyes. The Sphinx folds its wings, bows and settles into the sand.

    “I'd ask him a riddle back.”

    Guards a relic: the Scarab Amulet. A gold scarab amulet from Cairo's old treasures, guarded by the Sphinx beside the desert road. It carries you further down that road.
