The city

Gold and fog

In 1848 San Francisco was a small town of about 1,000 people. Then gold was found in the foothills, and by the end of 1849 some 25,000 lived there. The 1906 earthquake and fire left most of the city in ruins, and it was largely rebuilt by 1915.

“I'd pan for gold in the foothills, just once.”

The landmark

The orange bridge

When the Golden Gate Bridge opened in 1937, its 1,280-metre main span was the longest in the world, a record it held until 1964. Its orange colour was picked to suit the green hills and to stand out to ships in the fog.

Two keepers

Who keeps the wish in San Francisco.

An Elite wakes first; the Boss guards the wish itself. Hit their weak points for a BONK!, land the last blow for a KO!, and each one grins and takes a bow.

  • The Elite

    The Gripman

    Rings the bell on every hill · Keeper of the cable cars

    A gripman works the lever that grabs the moving cable under San Francisco's streets and pulls the car up the hills. Crews are famous for ringing their bells in rhythm, and the city has held a bell-ringing contest for them more than fifty times since 1949.

    The crab tips its cap, grips the lever and rings the bell twice. The crab lurches, then tightens its grip on the lever. The crab sets the lever down and rings the bell once.

    “I'd ask for a turn on the bell.”

  • The Boss

    Monarch

    The last king of the grizzlies · Keeper of the Golden State

    Monarch was a huge California grizzly, captured in 1889 for a newspaper stunt and kept for years in Golden Gate Park. California's grizzlies were hunted out not long after. Many say he was the model for the bear on the state flag, but no one can prove it.

    Monarch lifts his crowned head. Somewhere far off, the fog rolls back. Monarch rears up, growls and drops back onto all fours. Monarch lowers his crowned head and bows.

    “I'd tell him he's still on the flag.”

    Guards a relic: the Golden Gate Rivet. A rivet from the Golden Gate Bridge, guarded by Monarch in the fog. Like the bridge, it holds when you take one more knock.
