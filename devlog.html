Devlog

What's new in each version of Paper Skyline.

The road ahead · 6 October 2026

Where the plane goes next. Not in the app yet, and no dates.

Vision

Across the whole story of civilisation

Throw your paper plane across the cities and enjoy the wonders of our world. You won't only fly through today's skies, but across the whole story of human civilisation. Fly back in time to watch the Pyramids rise, or to London in the Victorian age. Fly ahead to a future where people voyage to the farthest stars. Then cross into fictional worlds and their cities to meet the characters you love. It's all waiting for you.

Version 1.6.2

New

Looks for your plane

Dress your plane in papers, colours, inks, folds, trails and decals, earned from flights, boxes and city clears.

New

Gifts and missions

Open gift boxes, fill a 7-day stamp card and take on three missions a day. Missions pay boxes, never coins.

New

A gift for our first flyers

Open Events and claim a red Paper Dragon, free, until 31 December 2026.

New

Bigger ad bonus

Watching an ad after a flight now pays at least $20.

Version 1.6.1

New

Smoother flying

Flights run at a steadier frame rate, with fewer hitches.

New

A cooler dawn

Morning light is softer and cooler, like an early start over the rooftops.

New

The spire in its place

The spire over Paris sits where it belongs again.

New

The nose follows the glide

As your plane glides down, its nose tips to follow the descent.

New

Walkers watch you fly

People in the street turn to watch your plane, stop to chat, and three new walkers join them.

New

Keepers fall

When you break a keeper, it now gets knocked down.

New

Solid skylines

Distant landmarks and bridges stay solid, softened by haze instead of fading.

Version 1.6.0

New

A gentler glide

Let go and your plane now drifts down slowly at a calm angle, then lifts its nose just before the ground.

New

A song for every city

Every city has its own music now, from an accordion café in Paris to jazz under the Golden Gate. Each track plays all the way through.

New

One calm level

City themes and the menu music now play at the same gentle volume.

New

Smoother skylines

The far skylines behind Rio de Janeiro and London scroll smoothly again.

New

Morning to evening everywhere

Like Paris, nine more cities now pass from morning light to evening as you fly.

Version 1.5.2

New

Language at a glance

The title and Settings now show your language as a flag. Tap it to pick another.

New

An Exit button

On Android, Exit now sits on the title and in Settings.

New

Walkers in front of the stand

At the start of a flight, passers-by now walk in front of the launch stand, not behind it.

New

Cleaner walkers

The people walking by no longer have a white outline.

New

The Eiffel Tower at night

The Eiffel Tower glows properly after dark, in two tones of gold.

New

Your language from the start

On a Vietnamese phone, Paper Skyline now opens in Vietnamese. You can still change it in Settings.

Version 1.5.1

New

Tiếng Việt

Paper Skyline now speaks Vietnamese, story and all. Pick Tiếng Việt in Settings.

New

Paris through the day

Fly Paris again and again and the light changes with the time of day.

New

A new Paris street

Paris has a new street to fly over, with fresh pavement and a new road.

New

People out walking

Paris's passers-by now walk with swinging arms, and a father and daughter have joined them.

New

The carousel horse gallops

The carousel horse now gallops when it wakes.

New

Smoother keepers

The keepers' idle animations are smoother.

Version 1.5.0

New

A new home screen

The game now opens at the window: the city outside, the wordmark in the sky and your plane ready to throw.

New

The streets notice you

People on the pavement look up when your plane flies low, and react where it lands. Paris also has a horse-drawn calèche.

New

Trees in the wind

Street trees now sway in passing gusts, and Paris has eleven new kinds of trees.

New

Keepers that move

Every keeper now moves while it waits for your plane, from the carousel horse to the kabuki actor.

New

No more skimming

Planes no longer skim along the rooftops. If you bought the Skim part, its coins came back to you.

Version 1.4.3

New

Replays keep your journey

Replaying an earlier city no longer moves your journey back to it. Continue always takes you to the newest city.

New

Continue from the Atlas

The city you are working on now has a Continue button on its Atlas page, and the globe stays on it.

New

Progress moves with your phone

On Android, your progress now goes into your phone's own backup, so a new phone can pick up where you left off.

Version 1.4.2

New

Every landing finishes

On some phones a flight could keep sliding forward and never end. Every landing now finishes.

Version 1.4.1

New

A friendlier first flight

Your first throw shows a ghost hand to follow. The results card says how much more your next upgrade needs, and Throw again is one tap away.

New

Change your mind before the throw

Before you throw, you can go back to the garage or the menu. It doesn't use up a flight.

New

Easier to read

The tips and buttons on the results card are bigger.

New

Streets that sit right

Cairo, Sydney and Rio's front rows stand on their quays, London's and Rome's landmarks are back, the clouds have soft bases and the Golden Gate is fixed.

New

A sidewalk for the taxi chase

The taxi fight's road now has a sidewalk on each side.

Version 1.4.0

New

Painted streets

London, Sydney, Beijing and Rome get streets painted like Paris: quays, rails, trees and pavements. And after Rio, all ten cities are yours to fly, San Francisco included.

New

Swipe and tap the taxi

In the taxi fight, swipe left or right to change lanes and tap anywhere to strike. No more buttons to aim for.

New

Every city keeps its run

Each city you've cleared keeps its own replay run: its wallet, upgrades and keepers stay where you left them.

New

A new skyline every flight

The buildings behind you rearrange on every flight, so no two tries look the same. The landmark, the keepers and the coins stay put.

New

Friendly tips

After your first flights, a short tip on the results card shows one thing to try next.

New

A flight cut short still pays

If your phone closes the game mid-flight, that flight now pays for how far it got when you come back.

Version 1.3.0

New

Tokyo, painted

Little houses along a stone-wall promenade, utility poles and their wires, and Fuji and the Skytree far off in the haze.

New

Cairo, painted

Sandstone houses by the Nile, with the Cairo Tower and the pyramids far off in the glow.

New

Rio, painted

Bright colonial houses on the wave pavement by the sea, with Sugarloaf rising over the bay.

New

A painted East River

New York's river is now painted water under the sunset.

New

Falling leaves in Paris

Painted plane-tree leaves tumble down as you fly over Paris.

New

A clock on Hard

On Hard, Elite and Boss fights run against a clock. On Easy and Medium a fight lasts until you break the keeper or run out of strikes.

New

The Stryge breathes

Paris's Boss now breathes slowly while it waits for you.

New

Garage tabs

The garage has tabs: Plane for upgrades, Relics to wear what your keepers left behind, and Records for your best flights and career.

Version 1.2.0

New

Paris, painted anew

Soft haze over the rooftops, the Seine and its bridges, and a quiet quay to glide along.

New

New York at sunset

Brownstones by the East River, autumn leaves, and the Empire State and Chrysler far off in the glow.

New

Clouds that billow

The clouds now billow as they drift by.

New

Hand-painted coins

Every coin along your way is now painted by hand.

New

The taxi's avenue

On a New York avenue, a yellow taxi keeper waits for a steady pilot. Turn your phone upright to meet it.

Version 1.1.0

New

Fly it again

Replay a city you've cleared with the plane you have now, and look for the treasures you missed.

New

Hard mode

A tougher way to fly each city: tighter arenas, and every keeper opens with an attack. A Hard clear earns a starred trophy.

New

A painted plane

Your plane is now painted by hand, and it changes as you upgrade: longer wings, taped and balsa-braced paper, a cardboard engine and its tank.

New

Treasures in the streets

Treasures now sit where they belong: on a painter's easel, an opera balcony, a subway step, a kite and a cable-car pylon.

New

The winged Sphinx

Cairo's Boss wakes up as a winged lion in black and gold. Watch its wings before you strike.

Version 1.0.0

New

Five cities to fly

Fly your paper plane over Paris, New York, Tokyo, Cairo and Rio de Janeiro. Each new city downloads once, then plays offline.

New

The porcelain keepers

Two souvenir keepers guard each city's wish. Fly into their arena, switch lanes and strike when a piece glints.

New

Draw a better plane

Spend what each flight earns in the garage: a stronger launcher, wider wings, tougher paper, an engine and a tank.

New

Hidden treasures

Rare finds hide in every city's streets. The Atlas gives you a riddle for each one you haven't found yet.

New

Easy or Medium

Pick how you want to fly on each city's card, before you throw.
