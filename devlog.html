Version 1.0.0

New

Five cities to fly

Fly your paper plane over Paris, New York, Tokyo, Cairo and Rio de Janeiro. Each new city downloads once, then plays offline.

New

The porcelain keepers

Two souvenir keepers guard each city's wish. Fly into their arena, switch lanes and strike when a piece glints.

New

Draw a better plane

Spend what each flight earns in the garage: a stronger launcher, wider wings, tougher paper, an engine and a tank.

New

Hidden treasures

Rare finds hide in every city's streets. The Atlas gives you a riddle for each one you haven't found yet.

New

Easy or Medium

Pick how you want to fly on each city's card, before you throw.
