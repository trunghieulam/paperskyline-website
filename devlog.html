Devlog

What's new in each version of Paper Skyline. The latest one is on Google Play.

The road ahead · 6 October 2026

Where the plane goes next. Not in the app yet, and no dates.

Vision

Across the whole story of civilisation

Throw your paper plane across the cities and enjoy the wonders of our world. You won't only fly through today's skies, but across the whole story of human civilisation. Fly back in time to watch the Pyramids rise, or to London in the Victorian age. Fly ahead to a future where people voyage to the farthest stars. Then cross into fictional worlds and their cities to meet the characters you love. It's all waiting for you.

Version 1.9.2

New

Four new missions

Daily missions now ask you to get people to notice you, hit a top speed, fly clean without a bump, or catch coins in a row.

Fix

No ad on the landed card

A cleared city or a medal no longer shows an ad on its card.

Fix

The landed plane sits behind the walkers

After landing, the plane is drawn behind people on the street, with its trail underneath.

Version 1.9.1

Fix

Day 7 can be claimed again

The Day 7 card on the Daily gifts board has its Take button back, so the seventh stamp claims its gift and a new board begins.

Fix

Rockets stay in place in a dive

In a nose-down roll, the plane's rockets no longer show as a flat copy over the top of the plane.

Fix

Your colour on the Dragon and the Gyro

The Paper Dragon and the Paper Gyro now fly in the colour shown in the Garage.

Version 1.9.0

New

A Paper Pop look

The title screen, the Workshop, the landing card, the Gifts page and the Daily gifts board wear a bright new Paper Pop look: sticker buttons, round icons and a city chip on Continue.

New

Gifts open in colour

Opening a gift fills the room with its tier's colour. Each tap can raise the tier, and a Rich gift bursts with RICH!

New

Boss fights hit harder

Weak-point strikes on a Boss land with BONK!, and the final strike freezes, bursts and ends with KO! and a dizzy keeper.

New

Keepers introduce themselves

The first time you enter an arena, its Elite or Boss introduces itself on a torn notebook page with its name and a short story.

New

Missions on one screen

Missions and Gifts fit on one screen. Accept missions before a flight, and open a finished mission's gift right from the landed card.

New

Open one, or stop

Each kind of gift on the Gifts page has its own Open 1. Taking them all opens them in turn, and Stop keeps the rest for later.

New

Prices on every part

Each Workshop part shows the price of its next step, and a red dot marks the ones you can buy now. The Workshop button before a throw carries the dot too.

New

What the flight earned

After landing, each row's pay hops into a This flight earned callout, then into your wallet. Bars show your best for the visit.

Fix

Only the city you fly to downloads

Choosing a city downloads that city alone, so it arrives sooner. Other cities wait until you choose them.

Version 1.8.6

New

Records clear of their bars

On tablets, the landed card's records, like best here and NEW BEST, no longer overlap their bars.

Version 1.8.5

New

First missions

Four first missions lead you to your first relics: hit a keeper in Paris, break the Neko, bring home the Koban and wear it, each for a box. Treasures now drop by chance, certain by the third break.

New

Wind up high

From Beijing on, a headwind blows above 60 m and reaches full strength at 140 m. Gusts and the odd leaf drift past, and an arrow by the dials shows its strength. Fly lower to keep your speed and fuel.

New

Sharper planes

The plane in the Garage flies a slow figure-eight, smaller and sharper, and follows every look you try, Ink included. Dressed planes look sharper in flight too.

New

Colours on Carbon

Carbon paper takes a Colour, in a deep shade that shows on the dark paper.

New

Tidier numbers and fixes

Effects read compact, such as +2s fuel. The looks grid keeps its place when you tap a tile, the reward line after landing wraps instead of being cut, and translations read better.

New

Coloured difficulty

Easy, Medium and Hard each have their own colour, green, amber and red, on the map, the city pages and the flight picker.

New

A new city-clear card

Clearing a city now shows a paper card like the landing one: what the flight earned, the reward, and one button to choose the next wish or open the Workshop.

Version 1.8.4

New

Five new cities

Fly over Istanbul, Hong Kong, Toronto, Bangkok and Barcelona. Each has its own landmark, streets, Elites and Boss, and downloads once.

New

Looks, Relics and Properties

The Garage opens on Looks, Relics and Properties. Properties shows your plane's stats at the start of a flight and at their best, the coin pickup radius, relic slots and every worn relic effect.

New

Relics you can grow

Beat a keeper again on Medium or Hard for a chance at another copy of its relic, then merge copies on the Garage bench to raise its level. Each relic shows its effect as a number.

New

Records in the Atlas

A Records page in the Atlas gathers your career totals and every city's best flight and medals, and each city page shows its medals.

New

Richer trails

Soft Rain falls as slanted rain, Confetti sheds flipping pieces and Fine Sand streams loose grains.

New

Patterned paper fits

Patterned paper lines up across the wings and fins, and shows its pattern in the Garage while it rolls.

New

Garage crash fixed

Trying on one patterned paper after another in the Garage no longer runs the phone out of memory and closes the game.

Version 1.7.4

New

Continue a city you left

Choosing New flight on the title no longer loses the run you were on: that city keeps its coins and parts, and its Atlas page and the globe offer Continue.

New

No ink

The Ink slot gains No ink, owned from the start, for a plane without ink lines. The default paper now reads Plain paper (default).

New

Ten wishes at the end

The end of the tour now counts ten wishes and ten cities.

New

Event titles that fit

A long event title on the title screen shrinks to fit before it is shortened, so Japanese titles read in full.

Version 1.7.3

New

Smaller, livelier trails

Trail pieces are smaller now. They tumble, drift and flutter behind your plane, then fade.

New

$15 and $20 coins

Coins can now be worth $8, $10, $15 or $20, and Lucky coins pay $15 or $20.

New

Text that fits

Japanese, Korean and Chinese text fits better: the title's Atlas row, Atlas cards and keeper names in the arena.

New

A gift in every language

The founder gift's banner now reads in every language the game speaks.

New

Accept and Take

Missions in Gifts start counting when you tap Accept, and a finished one pays its box when you tap Take. A finished mission stays ready to take after the daily or weekly reset.
