Devlog

Version 1.6

What's new in Paper Skyline 1.6.0 to 1.6.3, every update, newest first. The latest version is on Google Play.

Version 1.6.3

New

A gift box every day

Daily stamps now give a gift box each day, with a Rare box on days 4 and 7. The optional ad adds a second box.

New

Your looks

The Looks tab shows the looks you own.

New

Cairo holds steady

Cairo's pyramid stays in place as you dive.

Version 1.6.2

New

Looks for your plane

Dress your plane in papers, colours, inks, folds, trails and decals, earned from flights, boxes and city clears.

New

Gifts and missions

Open gift boxes, fill a 7-day stamp card and take on three missions a day. Missions pay boxes, never coins.

New

A gift for our first flyers

Open Events and claim a red Paper Dragon, free, until 31 December 2026.

New

Bigger ad bonus

Watching an ad after a flight now pays at least $20.

Version 1.6.1

New

Smoother flying

Flights run at a steadier frame rate, with fewer hitches.

New

A cooler dawn

Morning light is softer and cooler, like an early start over the rooftops.

New

The spire in its place

The spire over Paris sits where it belongs again.

New

The nose follows the glide

As your plane glides down, its nose tips to follow the descent.

New

Walkers watch you fly

People in the street turn to watch your plane, stop to chat, and three new walkers join them.

New

Keepers fall

When you break a keeper, it now gets knocked down.

New

Solid skylines

Distant landmarks and bridges stay solid, softened by haze instead of fading.

Version 1.6.0

New

A gentler glide

Let go and your plane now drifts down slowly at a calm angle, then lifts its nose just before the ground.

New

A song for every city

Every city has its own music now, from an accordion café in Paris to jazz under the Golden Gate. Each track plays all the way through.

New

One calm level

City themes and the menu music now play at the same gentle volume.

New

Smoother skylines

The far skylines behind Rio de Janeiro and London scroll smoothly again.

New

Morning to evening everywhere

Like Paris, nine more cities now pass from morning light to evening as you fly.
