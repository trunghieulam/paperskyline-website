Devlog

Version 1.9.0

What's new in Paper Skyline 1.9.0. The latest version is on Google Play.

New

A Paper Pop look

The title screen, the Workshop, the landing card, the Gifts page and the Daily gifts board wear a bright new Paper Pop look: sticker buttons, round icons and a city chip on Continue.

New

Gifts open in colour

Opening a gift fills the room with its tier's colour. Each tap can raise the tier, and a Rich gift bursts with RICH!

New

Boss fights hit harder

Weak-point strikes on a Boss land with BONK!, and the final strike freezes, bursts and ends with KO! and a dizzy keeper.

New

Keepers introduce themselves

The first time you enter an arena, its Elite or Boss introduces itself on a torn notebook page with its name and a short story.

New

Missions on one screen

Missions and Gifts fit on one screen. Accept missions before a flight, and open a finished mission's gift right from the landed card.

New

Open one, or stop

Each kind of gift on the Gifts page has its own Open 1. Taking them all opens them in turn, and Stop keeps the rest for later.

New

Prices on every part

Each Workshop part shows the price of its next step, and a red dot marks the ones you can buy now. The Workshop button before a throw carries the dot too.

New

What the flight earned

After landing, each row's pay hops into a This flight earned callout, then into your wallet. Bars show your best for the visit.

Fix

Only the city you fly to downloads

Choosing a city downloads that city alone, so it arrives sooner. Other cities wait until you choose them.
