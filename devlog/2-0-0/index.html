Devlog

Version 2.0.0

What's new in Paper Skyline 2.0.0. The latest version is on Google Play.

New

Three Stages in every city

Every city now has three Stages, each with tougher keepers. Your Easy, Medium and Hard clears count as Stage 1 done.

New

Émile, Imani and Hana

Meet Émile in Paris, Imani in New York and Hana in Tokyo. Their quests open Stages 2 and 3 and change their cities.

New

The Unfinished Song

Play The Unfinished Song across Paris and New York.

New

Menus as notebook pages

Menus are notebook pages. The Chapter page paints each city as you go, and Missions lists tasks for every city.

New

Relics from quests

Relics now come from quests, and the globe shows how ready your plane is for each city.
