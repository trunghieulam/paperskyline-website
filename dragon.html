A red Paper Dragon for our first flyers

A folded-paper dragon, gliding wings out, to wear on your plane. It is free, it is yours to keep, and there is a limited number of them.

Free while gifts last

What it is

The Paper Dragon is a look for your plane: red paper, gold edges and a long folded tail. It changes how your plane looks, nothing else, so it never makes a flight easier or harder. Once it is yours, find it in the Looks tab of the Garage and put it on any time.

How to claim it

  1. Update. Get the latest Paper Skyline from Google Play.
  2. Open it online. The game needs to be connected to check that a gift is still left.
  3. Tap Claim. Open Events and tap Claim on the red Paper Dragon.

Gifts last until they are all claimed. Anyone who has already claimed the dragon keeps it for good.
