The story

The lamp clicks on.

A boy by the window writes a wish on a notebook page, folds it into a paper plane and lets it go. The glass melts into ink and watercolour, and suddenly the plane is over Paris.

Every flight carries the wish a little farther. And somewhere above each city, something wakes up to meet it.

Stories in the cities

Some cities keep a story.

Fly far enough and someone will have one to share: a song left unfinished, lanterns waiting for the river. How it ends is yours to find.

  • Paris · New York

    The Unfinished Song

  • Tokyo

    Floating Lanterns

    An old lantern workshop, and a ceremony coming.

The road ahead

Where the plane goes next.

Throw your paper plane across the cities and enjoy the wonders of our world. You won't only fly through today's skies, but across the whole story of human civilisation. Fly back in time to watch the Pyramids rise, or to London in the Victorian age. Fly ahead to a future where people voyage to the farthest stars. Then cross into fictional worlds and their cities to meet the characters you love. It's all waiting for you.

That is where we are headed, not what is in the app today. Today there are twenty-one cities of the present day, and each update carries the plane a little farther.

The keepers

Every city keeps its wish behind a keeper.

Some are carved from stone, some honk their way down the avenue, and each one comes from its city's history and legends. Nobody gets hurt: find the way past, and they bow out with a grin.

Between flights

Draw a better plane.

Every flight pays for the next one. Spend it at the drawing board, and tomorrow's plane outflies today's.

There are gifts to open, looks to try on, and a few keepsakes worth flying a long way for.

Minigames

Some keepers ask for a hand.

Not every keeper wants a chase. A few have a puzzle they can't solve alone, and once you've helped, it waits on the Minigames page for another go.

In the game

Every city, painted.

Cherry blossom over Tokyo, a yellow cab under the Manhattan skyline. Each city has its own light and its own song.

Plainly

No account, nothing to buy

Your progress stays on your device. Paris comes with the app, and each other city downloads when you choose to fly there, with nothing about you sent. Anonymous gameplay stats help us balance the game, and you can switch them off. The only ad is one you choose to watch, for bonus cash on Android. The full policy is short.

Landscape, two thumbs

Drag back to throw. In the air, climb, dive and boost with the buttons under your thumbs.

Easy, Medium or Hard

Pick how hard the keepers push back on each city's card, and fly any cleared city again.
