Paper Skyline

iPhone, iPad and Android

Fold a wish. Fly it there.

A boy who can't go anywhere yet folds each wish into a paper plane and throws it through his window. Fly it over five cities, upgrade it between flights, and wake the porcelain keepers that guard each wish.

The first five wishes

Every city keeps its wish behind porcelain.

Each city guards its wish with two crackle-glazed souvenirs. One wakes first; the second keeps the wish itself. They are keepsakes, not enemies: nobody gets hurt, and they take a bow as they break.

How a flight goes

Throw, fly, upgrade.

Every flight pays for the next one, and every upgrade takes you farther across the city.

1.

Draw back and let go

Drag back from the launcher and release. How far you drag sets the power, and the angle sets the launch.

2.

Climb, dive, boost

Two thumbs steer the plane. Every flight pays coins for how far, how high, how long and how fast you flew, and for everything you knock over on the way.

3.

Land, then upgrade

Between flights the garage turns into a blueprint of your plane. Spend your coins, then throw again, a little farther than before.

The garage

Draw a better plane.

At night the boy redesigns his plane in the back of his notebook, and the garage is that drawing sheet: a stronger launcher, wider wings, tougher paper, a tiny engine and a tank to feed it. Tap a part to preview it, and tap its price to buy it.

Every new city starts with a fresh plane and an empty wallet, so the plane you build for Tokyo is not the one you'll want in Cairo.

The tour

Five cities, five wishes.

Each wish comes from his landmark books, and each city is painted around its landmark: the Eiffel Tower, the Empire State Building, the Tokyo Skytree with Fuji behind it, the pyramids of Giza and Sugarloaf Mountain. Every city has its own music.

Keep an eye out for hidden treasures. They are rare, they belong to the city's streets and landmarks, and the Atlas gives you a riddle for each one you haven't found. You keep every treasure you find, and each cleared wish earns a stamp on the paper globe.

How a city ends

Every city's wish is guarded by porcelain keepers: a carousel horse, a mime, a yellow taxi, a lucky cat and more. An Elite wakes first, then the Boss. Fly into its arena, switch lanes and strike when a piece glints.

Break the Boss and the wish comes true. Nobody gets hurt: the keepers crack, grin and take a bow.

In the game

Inked first, then washed with colour.

Plainly

No ads, no account, nothing to buy

Your progress stays on your device. Paris comes with the app, and New York, Tokyo, Cairo and Rio download once, with nothing about you sent. The full policy is short.

Landscape, two thumbs

Drag back to throw. In the air, climb, dive and boost with the buttons under your thumbs.

Easy or Medium

Pick a mode on each city's card. On Easy the keepers' attacks only knock a few coins loose; on Medium their weak points hide and they fight back. A flight that goes badly costs you the flight, and the cracks you made stay.
