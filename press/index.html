Press kit

Paper Skyline is a relaxed paper plane game for phones and tablets. A boy who can't travel yet writes a wish on a notebook page, folds it into a paper plane and throws it out of his window, and the skyline outside redraws itself, in ink and then watercolour, into the city of the wish. You fly that plane over twenty-one painted cities, from Paris to Athens, earn dream coins for distance, height and time in the air, and redraw the plane on a blueprint after every flight.
