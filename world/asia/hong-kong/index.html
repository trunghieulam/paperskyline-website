Hong Kong

Hong Kong is a special administrative region of China, on the south coast where the Pearl River meets the sea. It is a peninsula and more than 250 islands, and most of its land is green hills and country parks.

“I'd have waved at the planes from a Kowloon roof.”

  • Until 1998, airliners landing at the old Kai Tak airport made a sharp turn low over the rooftops of Kowloon.
  • Hong Kong's new airport was built on Chek Lap Kok, an island that was flattened and made bigger to hold the runways.
