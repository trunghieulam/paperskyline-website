Japan

Japan is a long chain of islands off the east coast of Asia. In 2023 a recount using digital maps found 14,125 of them, about twice the old figure. Its own name, Nihon, is often translated as "origin of the sun".

“I'd count its islands from the plane window.”

  • Mount Fuji, 3,776 metres, is Japan's highest mountain, and a volcano: it last erupted in 1707.
  • Japan counts 111 active volcanoes, and its weather agency watches dozens of them day and night.
