Thailand

Thailand lies in the middle of mainland Southeast Asia, between the Gulf of Thailand and the Andaman Sea. It is the only country in the region that was never ruled by a European power. Its flag has red, white and blue stripes.

“I'd fly as low as that first plane did.”

  • In 1911 the Belgian pilot Charles Van den Born made the first aeroplane flights in Siam, over a racecourse in Bangkok.
  • Every year on 13 March, Thailand celebrates Thai Elephant Day.
