United States

The United States spans North America from the Atlantic to the Pacific, with Alaska in the far north-west and Hawaii far out in the ocean. Its fifty states share one flag, with a star for each.

“I'd fly New York to Paris, just backwards.”

  • On 17 December 1903 Orville Wright flew for 12 seconds near Kitty Hawk, North Carolina: the first controlled, powered aeroplane flight.
  • In May 1927 Charles Lindbergh flew alone and non-stop from New York to Paris in about 33 and a half hours.
