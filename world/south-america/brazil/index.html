Brazil

Brazil is the largest country in South America, covering nearly half the continent. About 60 per cent of the Amazon rainforest lies inside it, and it is the only country in the Americas where Portuguese is the main language.

“I'd follow the Amazon all the way to the sea.”

  • On 23 October 1906 Alberto Santos-Dumont flew his 14-bis about 60 metres in Paris: the first powered flight watched by a public crowd.
  • Brazil shares a border with every country in South America except two: Chile and Ecuador.
